AGRICULTURE DIVISION CIRCULAR NO.14/2026
CHECK ON HACLHM MENU IN CBS FOR KCC, KSY AND SHG SCHEMES
Master Circulars on Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme and PNB Samooh Plus Scheme
for Financing Self Help Groups (SHGs) have been issued vide Agriculture Division
Circular No. 36/2025 dated 04-04-2025 and Agriculture Division Circular No. 95/2025
dated 11-09-2025 respectively. Detailed guidelines on Kisan Samriddhi Yojana (KSY)
were issued vide Agriculture Division Circular No. 108/2024 dated 03-08-2024 and
subsequent circulars issued from time to time.
Further, guidelines on review and renewal of KCC, KSY and SHG accounts are already
conveyed vide Agriculture Division Circular No.90/2025 dated 09-09-2025 and CRMD
Division Circular No.30/2023 dated 04-07-2023 and subsequent circulars issued from
time to time.
In terms of the above-mentioned Circulars “Renewal process of Borrower Accounts
(Exposure up to Rs. 25.00 Crore) through PNB LenS under the above-mentioned
schemes is Mandatory and Marking of Renewal in CBS will be allowed through PNB
LenS. CBS will not allow marking of RENEW option under menu HACLHM for Borrower
Accounts having Exposure up to Rs. 25.00 Crore.”
In order to comply with the bank guidelines, a system check has been implemented in
CBS, HACLHM menu for KCC, KSY and SHG accounts to encourage review and
renewal through PNB LenS only.
Further, for handling exceptions a menu RENRELAX has been made available in CBS.
All other guidelines shall remain unchanged.
All field functionaries are advised to ensure meticulous compliance of the guidelines.
