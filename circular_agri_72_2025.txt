AGRICULTURE DIVISION CIRCULAR NO.72/2025
MODIFICATIONS IN KISAN SAMRIDDHI SCHEME -
FINANCING TO BORROWERS WHO HAVE AVAILED LIMITS FROM PRIMARY
AGRICULTURE COOPERATIVE CREDIT SOCIETIES (PACS)
1. Detailed guidelines on Kisan Samriddhi Yojana (KSY) have been circulated vide
Agriculture Division Circular No. 108/2024 dated 03-08-2024 and subsequent
guidelines vide circulars issued from time to time.
2. On the basis of references received from field regarding modifications in the Kisan
Samriddhi Yojana with respect to sanctioning of fresh facilities or enhancement or
renewal of existing facilities to farmer borrowers who have already availed short term
loans from Primary Agriculture Cooperative Credit Societies, certain amendments in
the captioned Scheme have been approved by Competent Authority.
S.No. Existing Guidelines Revised Guidelines
01 Agriculture Division
Circular No. 108/2024
dated 03-08-2024
Clause 25. Point (d) of
25
Defaulter farmers/
members of Primary
Agricultural Cooperative
Societies will not be
eligible for loan.
Branches may extend KSY facility to farmer
members of Co-operative Societies who have
already availed short term crop loans from Primary
Agriculture Cooperative Credit Societies (PACS)
subject to following conditions:
a) The borrower should not be a defaulter member
of PACS at the time of sanctioning of fresh
facilities and enhancement or renewal of existing
loans.
b) KSY can be sanctioned/enhanced after
deduction of loan availed from PACS.
c) Limit sanctioned in such cases should not be
below ₹2 lakh from our Bank.
