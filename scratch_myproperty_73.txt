1. PURPOSE
For all purpose(s).
(Borrower to disclose specific purpose of loan and also to give an
undertaking that the loan shall be used for lawful/legitimate purposes.)

speculative (Speculation in stock, shares, securities, commodities, bullion etc) &
prohibitive purposes as restricted by Law.)
2. ELIGIBILITY: Individual, Joint owners are also eligible, having immovable property
to be mortgaged and sufficient income to repay the loan. (CIF/ Customer ID of
the borrower be created under Retail in CBS and should not be under
Corporate to classify the loan under Retail)
2.1 Loan can also be granted to:
Sole proprietor in personal/Individual capacity against the property standing in the
name of Proprietorship firm and occupied by proprietor.

i. For salaried: Minimum net monthly salary of Rs.25,000/-

(for last 3 years)

may be added back for the purpose of arriving at Net Income/profit.

consideration for the purpose of servicing of EMI or interest (for determining loan

only two (maximum) co-borrowers are permitted.
b. Income of any number of joint owners of property can be taken into consideration
for determining loan amount. However, in such cases also, maximum two co-
borrowers are permitted in addition to co-owners of the property.

10. RESTORATION OF LIMIT:
i. Bank, at the request of the borrower, may consider restoration of the original limit/
enhancement of the limit after the lapse of 36 months, where the conduct of the
account is satisfactory, i.e., borrower has shown good repayment track record
during the tenure of loan; has adequate repayment capacity and adequate ‘Margin’
is available. The restoration/ enhancement, if considered, shall, however, be
subject to present Realizable value of the property (to be arrived at as per Bank’s
extant guidelines), repayment capacity & other Terms &Conditions of the Scheme.
ii. Fresh repayment period subject to maximum of 180 months may be permitted at
the time of restoration/ enhancement of OD limit after 36 months. Subject to
fulfilment of age, eligibility and compliance to all Terms & Condition.
iii. However, restoration/ enhancement of Overdraft limit be done as per the extant
guidelines of this scheme.
Note: Restoration of limit to be done by Branch/PLPs and above, within their vested
loaning power.
11. REPAYMENT PERIOD:
11.1 Overdraft – Overdraft on reducing DP basis to be liquidated in 15 years OR up to
the age of 70 years, whichever is earlier, subject to regular source of income.
Drawing power will be reduced on monthly basis to the extent of the principal
component of the EMI calculated on full sanctioned limit, so that the OD limit is
liquidated at the end of the loan tenure. Restoration of limit is permitted after 3 years
of satisfactory conduct of the account subject to eligibility criteria at the time of
restoration.
11.2 Term Loan- Loan amount together with interest to be repaid in maximum 180
Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) OR up to the age of 70, whichever is earlier,
subject to regular source of income.
Note: Incumbent PLPs/CHCAC and above may consider repayment tenure
according to the age of co-borrower who is not a co-owner, maximum upto the age
of 70 years or 15 years whichever is earlier. In such cases the owner of the
property to be made primary borrower irrespective of his/her age.
