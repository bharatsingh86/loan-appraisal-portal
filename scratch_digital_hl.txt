scrutinize all documents including legal report, valuation, visit/
inspection of property.
2. Branch to ensure that joint Borrower(s)/Co- applicant (s) will
be strictly as per Bank’s extant Home Loan product.
3. Security Inspection/Visit.
4. Obtention of NEC/Legal Search Report of property, to be taken
as security from Bank’s empanelled advocate, in terms of
Bank’s extant guidelines.
5. Obtention of Valuation report of property from Bank’s
empanelled valuer, in terms of Bank’s extant guidelines.
6. Before disbursement branch has to ensure the genuineness of
the documents.
7. Loan documents including acceptance of sanction shall be
executed at branch in terms of extant guidelines.
8. Branch wherein home loan account is parked, needs to take
print outs of e-Sanction letter, CIC report, undertaking, ITRs
account statement, KYC Documents from the portal and these
are to be kept on record for Audit purpose.
