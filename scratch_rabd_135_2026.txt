CELEBRATE DREAMS, CELEBRATE LIFE
FROM 01.10.2026 TO 31.12.2026
The retail lending portfolio continues to be a key contributor to the Bank’s growth by
generating stable income, diversifying credit risk and fostering long-term customer
relationships. With increasing financial awareness and sustained demand across the
housing, automobile, education and personal finance sectors, the retail loan segment,
particularly Home Loans, Mortgage Loans, Car Loans, Education Loans and other
retail credit products, offers significant growth potential.
The October–December quarter coincides with the festive season across the country,
encompassing major festivals such as Navratri/Durga Puja, Dussehra, Diwali, Guru
Nanak Jayanti and Christmas, along with various other festivals and occasions
observed by different communities. The festive season generally witnesses increased
consumer activity and demand for housing, automobiles, personal requirements and
other retail financing needs, providing an opportunity to enhance customer
engagement, generate quality leads and accelerate growth in the retail loan portfolio.
2. To accelerate growth in the retail loan portfolio through focused marketing
initiatives and an enhanced customer experience, the Bank has launched “PNB Retail
Loan Campaign – PNB Utsav Festivities 2026” from 01.10.2026 to 31.12.2026.
During the campaign period, the Bank aims to achieve tangible growth under the Core
Retail Loan segment, excluding Loans Against Deposits (LAD).
The campaign aims to achieve Rs 24,825 Cr of fresh sanctions under the Retail Loan
segment during the campaign period as under:
i. Rs 10,000 Crore for Home Loan
ii. Rs 5,000 Crore for Car Loan
iii. Rs 4,000 Crore for Mortgage Loans (including Top-up)
iv. Rs 2,000 Crore for Personal Loan
v. Rs. 3,825 Crore for other Retail Loans
