CENTRAL SECTOR SCHEME FOR FINANCING FACILITY UNDER AGRICULTURE
INFRASTRUCTURE FUND – AIF SCHEME
Detailed guidelines on Central Sector Scheme for Financing Facility under Agriculture
Infrastructure Fund have been circulated vide Agriculture Division Circular No. 37/2022
dated 27-06-2022 and subsequent circulars issued from time to time.
Now, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), GoI has issued revised
guidelines on the captioned scheme. Certain amendments have been made in the
existing AIF guidelines and more activities have been added which can be covered under
the AIF Scheme.
Consolidated guidelines on the captioned scheme are enclosed as Annexure.
All field functionaries are advised to ensure meticulous compliance. All Inspecting
Officials are advised to note the guidelines to ensure its compliance. All concerned are
further advised to take possible steps to popularize the scheme and make concerted
efforts to garner fresh business under the scheme.

S. PARAMETERS DESCRIPTION
1) Objective of
Scheme
To mobilize a medium - long term debt financing facility for
investment in viable projects for post-harvest management
Infrastructure and viable farming assets through incentives
and financial support in order to improve agriculture
infrastructure in the country. This financing facility will fulfil
numerous objectives for all the stakeholders in the
agriculture eco-system.
2) Implementation
Period
The Scheme will be operational till 2032-33.
3) Eligible
Beneficiaries
a) Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), Marketing
Cooperative Societies, Farmer Producers Organizations
(FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHG), Farmers, Joint Liability
Groups (JLG), Multipurpose Cooperative Societies, Agri-
entrepreneurs, Startups and Central/State agency or
Local Body sponsored Public Private Partnership
Projects, State Agencies, Agricultural Produce Market
Committees (Mandis), National & State Federations of
Cooperatives, Federations of FPOs (Farmer Produce
Organizations) and Federations of Self Help Groups
(SHGs)
b) All business entities including companies, partnership
firms etc.
c) APMCs operating regulated markets for agriculture and
allied sector produce including fisheries shall also be
eligible.
d) PACS who have adopted digitization for handling its
operations will be given preference under this scheme.
e) Concerned Officials should ask for and verify the legal
documents/ MoU/ Agreements of eligible community
applying for financing facility under the said scheme in
order to ensure collective responsibility of the eligible
community.

29) Project
Management
and
handholding
support
An online platform will be made available by Govt. of India
and participating Banks to provide information and loan
sanctioning facility. Agri. Infra fund will be managed and
monitored through an online MIS platform. It will enable all the
qualified entities to apply for loan under the fund. The system
will also provide benefits such as transparency of interest
rates offered by multiple banks, scheme details including
interest subvention and credit guarantee offered, minimum
documentation, faster approval process as also integration
with other scheme benefits. At the back end, the platform will
also provide multiple views of dashboards across district;
state and national level PMUs to monitor the total sanctioned
amount and number of borrowers, total interest subvention
benefit availed, loan statement summary, demographic and
geographic mix of borrowers and type of projects.
Each of the States/ UTs will set-up PMUs in their respective
states for creation of awareness, identifying potential clusters,
mobilization of applications, review of all the stakeholders,
and providing all necessary handholding support under the
scheme.
Project reports with indicative unit costs will be prepared by
Central and State PMUs for guidance of beneficiaries and
lending entities. Such project reports shall be available on
online platform.

32) Handling of
Loan Accounts
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmer Welfare vide Circular
1005-17/2022-AIF dated 11-08-2023 had issued clarification
with respect to handling of AlF loan accounts where quantum
of loan exceeds the specified limit of ₹2 Crore under
Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) Scheme:
a) Two separate loan accounts are to be opened under
Scheme Code (TLAIF or TLIFS- where subsidy is
available) for the purpose of calculation of interest and
appropriation of subvention amount - one for loan amount
₹2 Crore and other for rest of loan amount.
b) When EMI / periodical loan installment is collected, it
should be proportionately distributed between both the
AIF and Non-AIF component on the basis of the quantum
of loan in each component.
c) As and when Interest subvention is received, such an
amount should be credited to the AlF component of the
Loan. Under no circumstances the AlF Interest subvention
should be appropriated to non-AlF component of the
Loan.
d) It is to be ensured that subvention benefits are available
in AIF loan accounts only upto 7 years.
