ANNEXURE
1. Clarification regarding eligible processing activities under the scheme:
Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare (DAC&FW), Ministry of
Agriculture & Farmers Welfare has clarified that for the purpose of the scheme, primary
processing relates to value addition to a raw agricultural produce which, after
processing, does not result in change of product form.
DAC&FW has provided a detailed crop-wise list of primary processing activities
(Appendix- I) which are eligible and not eligible under the AIF.
2. Clarification regarding inclusion of Nursery/Planting Material Production
infrastructure as eligible project under Central Sector Scheme of Financing
Facility under Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
DAC&FW has informed that for the purpose of AIF, community farming assets mean
assets which can be used by more than one farmer for farming activities. It also
includes assets and infrastructure which can be rented out to farmers.
In view of same the department has clarified that any nursery or planting material
infrastructure which is built for providing plants/ planting material to more than one
farmers in the area, even on commercial terms, is included in the definition of
community farming assets.
3. Geo Tagging of projects under Agriculture Infrastructure Fund
The projects financed under the scheme are to be geo-tagged. A mobile application
namely ‘Kisan Suvidha’ has already been made available by DAC&FW for the purpose
and the beneficiaries can geo-tag their projects through this app.
Branch Heads are advised to ask/ help beneficiaries, financed under the scheme, to
geo-tag their projects. Step-wise instructions on how to update the geo location
through this app is given in Appendix-II.

APPENDIX- II
INSTRUCTIONS FOR GEO TAGGING OF PROJECTS UNDER AIF
 The new version of the Kisan Suvidha App can be installed from Google Play
store from the link:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.cdac.bharatd.agriapp.
 The Beneficiaries can select under "Project Geo Tagging" for Auto updating of
Geo Coordinate of Projects and login using the same credentials issued from
the AIF portal on the Kisan Suvidha Mobile App and press the Update button in
the Project Address section.
 The Beneficiary just needs to be physically positioned at the project location to
accurately save their Project's location.
 The App has options to display Beneficiary details, Loan Application and the
current status. General information about the scheme is also available on the
Mobile App.
 A Help button is also placed next to Latitude Longitude field on the Loan
Application on the AIF portal. On click of button, the users can find the
instructions on how to update the project Geo-location through Kisan Suvidha
Mobile App.
